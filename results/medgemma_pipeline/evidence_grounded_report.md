# Evidence-grounded medical answer

## Answer

Wearing face masks can offer several health benefits. They can help reduce the spread of respiratory droplets that carry viruses like influenza and COVID-19, protecting both the wearer and those around them. Masks can also help to filter out some airborne particles, potentially reducing exposure to pollutants and allergens. However, it's important to note that masks can sometimes cause discomfort, such as skin irritation or breathing difficulties, especially in individuals with pre-existing respiratory conditions.
